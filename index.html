💌 A Letter For You

My Dearest Aari ❤️,

Agar tum ye letter padh rahi ho, to shayad is waqt tumhare chehre par ek chhoti si smile hogi. Aur agar waqai smile hai... to samajh lena ki meri mehnat successful ho gayi. Kyunki is website ka sirf ek hi purpose tha... tumhare chehre par smile lana.

Ye website sirf kuch code se nahi bani hai. Iska har page, har photo, har animation aur har line mere dil ka ek chhota sa hissa hai. Shayad ye duniya ki sabse perfect website na ho... lekin mere liye ye sabse special hai, kyunki ye sirf aur sirf tumhare liye bani hai.

Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon ki life kitni ajeeb hoti hai. Hazaron log milte hain, hazaron chehre dikhte hain... lekin un sab me se ek insaan aisa mil jata hai jo dheere dheere poori duniya ban jata hai. Mere liye wo insaan tum ho.

Mujhe nahi pata ki future me hum kitni jagah ghumenge, kitni photos lenge ya kitni aur memories banayenge. Lekin itna zaroor pata hai ki main chahta hoon meri har achhi memory me tum ho.

Jab bhi main humari photos dekhta hoon, mujhe sirf ek photo nahi dikhti. Mujhe wo poora din yaad aa jata hai. Humari baatein yaad aa jati hain. Tumhari hasi yaad aa jati hai. Aur fir bina wajah mere face par bhi smile aa jati hai.

Kabhi kabhi humari ladai hoti hai. Kabhi misunderstanding hoti hai. Kabhi hum dono gussa bhi ho jate hain. Lekin sach bataun... mujhe humari ladai se kabhi darr nahi lagta. Mujhe sirf ek cheez se darr lagta hai... aur wo hai tumhe khona.

Isliye agar kabhi main galti karun, to mujhe samjha dena. Agar kabhi tum galti karo, to main samajhne ki poori koshish karunga. Kyunki relationship jeetne ka naam nahi hota... relationship saath rehne ka naam hota hai.

Main promise nahi karta ki main perfect boyfriend ban paunga. Shayad kabhi overthink karunga, kabhi stupid harkatein bhi karunga, kabhi bina wajah possessive bhi ho jaunga. Lekin ek promise zaroor karta hoon... Main har din kal se better boyfriend banne ki koshish karunga.

Aur ek baat... Thank you. Meri life me aane ke liye... Meri hasi ki wajah banne ke liye... Aur mujhe itna pyaar dene ke liye. Tum meri life ka sabse beautiful chapter ho... aur main dil se chahta hoon ki ye chapter kabhi khatam na ho. ❤️

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki words kabhi bhi meri feelings ko poori tarah express nahi kar sakte. Main kitna bhi likh loon, kitna bhi bol loon... phir bhi lagta hai ki jo main tumhare liye feel karta hoon, wo usse kahin zyada hai.

Tumne shayad kabhi notice na kiya ho, lekin jab bhi tum khush hoti ho na, bina kisi wajah ke mera mood bhi achha ho jata hai. Aur jab tum udaas hoti ho, to dil karta hai ki kaash tumhara saara dard main le sakta.

Mujhe tumhari hasi pasand hai. Tumhari baatein pasand hain. Tumhara gussa bhi kabhi kabhi cute lagta hai. Aur sabse zyada mujhe ye pasand hai ki tum meri life ka hissa ho.

Ho sakta hai future me hum dono bahut busy ho jayein. Ho sakta hai kabhi kabhi milna mushkil ho. Ho sakta hai life hume test kare. Lekin ek promise main aaj bhi karta hoon...

Chahe situation koi bhi ho, main kabhi humare against nahi jaunga. Agar problem aayi, to main us problem ke against khada rahunga... tumhare against kabhi nahi.

Main chahta hoon ki jab bhi tumhe lage ki koi tumhe samajh nahi raha, tab tumhe yaad aaye ki ek insaan hai jo hamesha tumhari baat sunega. Jo tumhe judge nahi karega. Jo tumhara haath chhodne ke bajay aur mazbooti se pakdega.

Aur haan... agar kabhi future me hum buddhe ho gaye, baalon me safedi aa gayi, chehre par wrinkles aa gaye... tab bhi main isi tarah tumhe dekhkar smile karna chahta hoon.

Kyunki mere liye pyaar sirf aaj tak nahi hai... mera pyaar har aane wale kal ke liye hai.

Agar mujhe dobara life jeene ka mauka mile... aur dobara tumhe choose karne ka option mile... To main bina ek second soche... Har baar... Sirf tumhe hi choose karunga. ❤️

Ek aur baat jo shayad main kabhi itni achhi tarah bol nahi paaya... Tumne mujhe sirf pyaar nahi diya... tumne mujhe ek reason diya better insaan banne ka.

Jab bhi main future imagine karta hoon, usme sirf success ya paisa nahi dikhta... usme tum dikhti ho. Ek chhota sa ghar... bahut saari hasi... chhoti chhoti baatein... aur hum dono ek dusre ke saath.

Ho sakta hai life hamesha easy na ho. Ho sakta hai kabhi hum thak jayein, kabhi gussa ho jayein, kabhi ro bhi dein. Lekin meri ek hi wish hai... Kabhi bhi hum ek dusre ka haath mat chhodna.

Agar kabhi tumhe lage ki duniya tumhare against hai, to bas itna yaad rakhna... Main tumhare saath hoon. Aaj bhi. Kal bhi. Aur jab tak meri saanse chalengi... tab tak.

Thank you, Aari bby... Meri life me aane ke liye. Mere boring dinon ko special banane ke liye. Meri har smile ka reason banne ke liye. Aur mujhe ye ehsaas dilane ke liye ki sachcha pyaar sirf movies me nahi hota.

Agar kabhi future me hum ve website saath me baithkar dekhen... To main bas itna chahta hoon ki tum meri taraf dekhkar ek baar smile karo... Kyuki us ek smile ke liye hi maine ye sab banaya hai.

Aur agar tum yahan tak padh chuki ho... To shayad ab tum samajh gayi hogi ki ye sirf ek website nahi hai. Ye meri diary hai... Meri feelings hain... Aur meri taraf se tumhare liye ek chhota sa gift hai.

Ho sakta hai kuch saal baad hum is website ko dobara kholen aur has dein ki dekho, kitni simple si website thi... Lekin mujhe umeed hai ki us waqt bhi is website ki value kam nahi hogi. Kyuki isme sirf photos nahi... Isme humari yaadein hain. Sirf words nahi... Isme mera dil hai.

Main nahi jaanta future hume kahan le jayega... Lekin main itna zaroor jaanta hoon ki jab bhi main apni life ke sabse khoobsurat chapter ke baare me sochunga... Us chapter ka naam hamesha "Arti" hoga.

Thank you... Mujhe samajhne ke liye. Mere saath hasne ke liye. Mere saath ladne ke liye. Mujhe maaf karne ke liye. Aur sabse zyada... Meri life ka hissa banne ke liye.

Main tumse koi perfect relationship ka promise nahi karta. Main bas itna promise karta hoon... Ki har problem me tumhare saath khada rahunga. Har khushi me tumhare saath hasunga. Aur har mushkil me tumhara haath aur mazbooti se pakadunga.

Agar kabhi tumhe meri yaad aaye... Aur main paas na hoon... To bas ye website khol lena. Yahan tumhe meri har feeling mil jayegi. Har photo me meri smile hogi. Har line me mera pyaar hoga. Aur har heartbeat me sirf tumhara naam.

I love you more than yesterday... But less than tomorrow. Because every new day... Makes me love you even more.

Happy Girlfriend Day, My Love. ❤️ Forever Us. Forever You. Forever Me. Forever We. ❤️♾️
